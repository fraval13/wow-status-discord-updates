# WoW Status

**Your friends. Your adventure. Don’t miss their big moments.**

WoW Status shares your group’s milestones inside the game. Celebrate a friend’s
level-up, first dungeon clear or new mount with queued notifications, colourful
captions and reaction whispers. Keep the story in a shared activity log, even
when you weren’t online for every moment.

Everyone installs the addon and enters the same **group ID**. No companion app,
separate account or player-hosted server is required for in-game sharing.

## Celebrate the milestones

- Level-ups, deaths and first recorded zone visits.
- First dungeon clears, first raid boss kills, mount milestones and major class unlocks.
- Profession milestones, reputation promotions, PvP ranks and each 100 completed quests.
- The first time a character equips eligible rare, epic or legendary armor.
- One battleground win/loss report with killing blows, honorable kills, deaths,
  honor, damage and healing when the game makes those scoreboard totals available.

Ordinary quest turn-ins, looting and routine gear swaps stay quiet. Battleground
and arena deaths do not produce ordinary death announcements.

## Keep up with your group

Browse shared character profiles, equipment and available combat stats. Compare
progress on the group leaderboard, discover who reached milestones first, and
find earlier moments in the activity log.

Friends who are online can help recover missed group history when you log in.
Catch-up is quiet: old entries do not replay as popups. Map markers can show
online group members where the game makes their position available.

## Choose what you share and see

Each announcement choice controls both what your character shares and what you
see from friends, including log catch-up. Character details and live position
sharing have separate settings. Keep your **group ID private**: anyone who knows
it can join.

Choose caption personalities, reaction voices, themes and notification size.
Use the addon with a mouse, keyboard or controller. English and Norwegian
(Nynorsk) are available, including notification captions and reaction whispers.

## Getting started

1. Install the file for your WoW edition and log in.
2. Open **Options → AddOns → WoW Status → Groups**.
3. Enter the same group ID as your friends.
4. Click the minimap book or type `/wstatus` to open WoW Status.

Supports **Retail, Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and WoW Forever**, with a separate
file for each edition. Game APIs and realm, faction, instance and phase rules
can affect which information and deliveries are available.
