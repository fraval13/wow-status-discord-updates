Your warrior friend finally hits level 40. The mage learns a new profession. Someone equips that first epic. You might be on opposite sides of the world, but those little victories should still feel shared.

WoW Status brings your friends’ adventures into your game. Join the same group ID to get illustrated notifications and a shared activity log. Just the addon, right inside WoW.

A QUEST FOR YOU & YOUR FRIENDS

Three steps to set out together.

  1. 1

    Pick your world.

    Download the ZIP for your client: Vanilla Classic, TBC Anniversary, Retail, or Forever. Each is a separate addon build.

  2. 2

    Unpack your supplies.

    Close WoW. Extract the ZIP and copy the WoWStatus folder into your game’s Interface/AddOns directory. Start WoW and enable it in the character screen’s AddOns list.

    ▸ Interface / AddOns / WoWStatus
  3. 3

    Gather your friends.

    Open Options → AddOns → WoW Status → Groups, or type /wstatus. Add the same group ID as your friends. Your groups are shared across your account.

    YOUR SHARED GROUP IDthe-tavern-crewPick your own. Give that same ID to your friends.

Updating? Replace the addon folder and keep your SavedVariables to retain your groups, settings, and log.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE TAVERN

A few things worth knowing.

Does everyone need another application?

No. Everyone just installs the addon and joins the same group ID. The sharing and notifications happen inside WoW.

Do we need the same realm and faction?

For Vanilla Classic and TBC Anniversary, use the same realm and faction. Sharing depends on the game’s chat and addon-message rules, so a matching group ID cannot bypass those restrictions. Forever has its own separate build and still depends on the communication its client allows.

What happens while I’m offline?

Your log catches up automatically at login when an online group member has announcements you missed. It is a shared in-game history, not an always-online archive. Recovered entries go into the log without replaying a wave of old popups.

Will this fill my screen or spam my chat?

Notifications queue one at a time. Click to dismiss, choose the announcements you want in Options, and use the minimap button to browse the log later.

What about Retail and Forever?

Retail and Forever have separate builds with their own client adapters. Choose the edition for the game you play. Each edition has its own messages and history. Some milestones, including dungeon clears, require game signals that may not be available on every client.

How do I open the activity log?

Left-click the WoW Status minimap button. Right-click it for Options. You can also open the log from the main WoW Status settings page.
