WELCOME TO WOW STATUS

Your party is bigger than your party.

◆

Your warrior friend finally hits level 40. The mage learns a new profession. Someone equips that first epic. You might be on opposite sides of the world, but those little victories should still feel shared.

WoW Status brings your friends’ adventures into your game. Join the same group ID to get illustrated notifications and a shared activity log. Just the addon, right inside WoW.

◆THE MOMENTS THAT MATTER◆

Another level. Another celebration.

See your friends level up, unlock major class abilities, and learn their first mount.

That first glorious equip.

Rare, epic, and legendary armor, announced the first time each item is equipped.

A new craft to master.

First-learned professions and new titles like Journeyman, Expert, Artisan, and Master.

A world still worth exploring.

First recorded zone visits, each hundred completed quests, and reputation and PvP rank milestones.

The misadventures, too.

A fallen friend gets a moment of recognition. Some of the best stories start with a very bad pull.

A shared chronicle.

Scroll through the activity log and catch up automatically when you log in and another group member is online.

A GLIMPSE OF THE ADVENTURE

Little moments. A proper entrance.

Readable popups, matching icons, and a queue so every moment gets its turn. Click a popup to dismiss it; the story stays in your log.

THE WINDOW BEHIND THE POPUPS

Everyone’s story, in one window.

Popups come and go. Left-click the minimap button and the main window keeps the whole story: the day’s activity, a leaderboard for each group, and every character your friends share, grouped by player.

A QUEST FOR YOU & YOUR FRIENDS

Three steps to set out together.

  1. 1

    Pick your world.

    Install it with the CurseForge app, which keeps it up to date for the games it manages. Or download the ZIP for your client: Vanilla Classic, TBC Anniversary, Retail, or Forever. Each is a separate addon build, and a ZIP does not update itself.

  2. 2

    Unpack your supplies.

    Using the CurseForge app? It copies the files for you. With a ZIP, close WoW, extract the ZIP and copy the WoWStatus folder into your game’s Interface/AddOns directory. Either way, start WoW and enable it in the character screen’s AddOns list.

    ▸ Interface / AddOns / WoWStatus
  3. 3

    Gather your friends.

    Open Options → AddOns → WoW Status → Groups, or type /wstatus. Add the same group ID as your friends. Your groups are shared across your account.

    YOUR SHARED GROUP IDthe-tavern-crewPick your own. Give that same ID to your friends.

Updating? The CurseForge app does it for you where it manages your game. With a ZIP, replace the addon folder and keep your SavedVariables to retain your groups, settings, and log.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE TAVERN

A few things worth knowing.

Does everyone need another application?

No. Everyone just installs the addon and joins the same group ID. The sharing and notifications happen inside WoW.

Do we need the same realm and faction?

For Vanilla Classic and TBC Anniversary, use the same realm and faction. Sharing depends on the game’s chat and addon-message rules, so a matching group ID cannot bypass those restrictions. Forever has its own separate build and still depends on the communication its client allows.

What happens while I’m offline?

Your log catches up automatically at login when an online group member has announcements you missed. It is a shared in-game history, not an always-online archive. Recovered entries go into the log without replaying a wave of old popups.

Will this fill my screen or spam my chat?

Notifications queue one at a time. Click to dismiss, choose the announcements you want in Options, and use the minimap button to browse the log later.

What about Retail and Forever?

Retail and Forever have separate builds with their own client adapters. Choose the edition for the game you play. Each edition has its own messages and history. Some milestones, including dungeon clears, require game signals that may not be available on every client.

How do I open the activity log?

Left-click the WoW Status minimap button. Right-click it for Options. You can also open the log from the main WoW Status settings page.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER

What you share, and what stays home.

WoW Status shares your adventures with friends, so here is exactly what it sends, to whom, and how to stop. The same statement is in the addon: type /wstatus privacy, or open it from the setup guide.

What is sent

  • A note to each group you join, saying that you joined.
  • The milestones your announcement choices allow: level-ups, deaths, first zone visits, every 100 quests, profession and dungeon milestones, mounts, reputation, class abilities, PvP rank, first armor equips, and session recaps if you tick them. A death also names what killed you, and where when Location is ticked.
  • Your character list: the name, race, sex, class, level and XP progress of each character shown to friends, when it reached each tenth level, the player name you chose, a random player ID and your addon version. A hidden character’s name is still sent, marked as hidden, so friends’ lists leave it out, even after you stop sharing your list.
  • The character details you tick: played time, professions with skill levels, completed quests, gold and gold earned, monsters killed, dungeons, mounts and honor kills, total deaths, and the gear you wear with its combat stats. Friends see them change as you play.
  • The name of a group you tick as Listed, to the members of your other groups.
  • Only when you click: a reaction whisper such as “GZ!”, the homepage link from Share WoW Status, or an invitation to a group, with its name, to the friend you invite.

Who receives it

  • The members of your groups. Updates travel as the game’s own addon whispers to the members it finds in each group’s chat channel, and friends who are online pass them on to members who were offline.
  • Your log is part of this: when a member logs in, the group’s entries from the last 7 days that you sent or received are offered to them.
  • Anyone who enters the same group name becomes a member, so give it only to people you trust.
  • Whispers you click to send go to the player you click on, who may not be in a group with you.
  • Nothing goes to a website, to the author or to any server except the game’s own, which carries every whisper.

What stays on your computer

  • Screenshots, your caption personality and reaction voice (a reaction you click is whispered in that voice), your notification and window settings, quiet hours, muted friends, and log entries that went to no group.

What is never sent

  • Chat you type or read, mail, bag contents, your guild (even with Guild ticked), and which quests you hand in.
  • The only text you type that travels is your player name and the name of a group you list or invite someone to.

How to stop

  • Untick an announcement, or one of the character details listed above, in Options. Race, sex, class, level and XP progress travel with your character list whatever you tick, and the Faction tick changes nothing, as your race shows your faction.
  • Untick a character under Characters shown to friends: it leaves everyone’s character list, leaderboard and gear view, but its announcements still go out, and a death’s details carry your player ID.
  • Untick Share my character list on the Characters page: your list, details and gear stop, and your friends are told to forget your list.
  • Type /wstatus pause, or untick Share my progress, to stop this character’s announcements. Its character list entry and most ticked details, such as gold, kills and gear, keep going until you hide it or untick them as above.
  • Pausing deletes all of this character’s own entries from your log and its whole History / graph, for good. Unticking an announcement or detail deletes the saved entries of that kind.
  • Remove a group in /wstatus groups to leave it.

What is kept

  • Your log keeps the newest 2,000 of your own entries (at most 1,000 of them sent to no group) and 2,000 received ones.
  • Apart from screenshots, which are pictures in the game’s Screenshots folder, everything WoW Status saves is in WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/WoWStatus.lua, with the game’s backup copy WoWStatus.lua.bak beside it. Removing the addon folder deletes neither; delete both files while the game is closed.
  • Friends keep the updates they already received in their own log.
